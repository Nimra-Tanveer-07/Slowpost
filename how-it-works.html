How it works

Four steps. Most letters take ten minutes to write.

  1. Write

    Type your letter or paste it in. Add up to three photos on the Letter and Time Capsule plans.

  2. Choose a date and address

    Send it to yourself or to someone else. Pick the exact day it should land.

  3. We seal it

    Your letter is encrypted and stored. Nothing is printed until a few days before delivery.

  4. It arrives

    We print, stamp and post it so it lands on your date. If you have moved, send us the new address any time before then.
