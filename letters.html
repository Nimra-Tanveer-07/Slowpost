What people send

You can write anything, but these six are the ones we see most.

To future me

Goals, worries and a snapshot of an ordinary Tuesday.

Birthday in advance

A letter for your child, friend or parent that arrives on a birthday you may not be able to attend.

Time capsule

A family letter with photos, sealed for ten years and opened together.

Things I never said

The thank-you or apology you needed more time to word properly.

Anniversary

Write now, then surprise your partner on the day itself.

Graduation or first day

Encouragement from today for the person you will be on a big day.

Slowpost

Letters to the future, sealed today and delivered on the day you pick.
